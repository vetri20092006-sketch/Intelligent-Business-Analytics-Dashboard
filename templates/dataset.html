Data Dictionary & Schema Definitions

Technical feature documentation for ML engineers and analysts

Schema Specs
Feature Identifier Data Type Permitted Range / Choices Business Domain Significance
tenure_months Integer 1 to 72 months Active account longevity. Accounts in months 1-3 experience 3x higher risk than accounts with tenure > 18 months.
contract_type Categorical Month-to-Month, Annual, Multi-Year Renewal cadence. Month-to-month contracts lack switching barrier friction and exhibit elevated churn volatility.
contract_length_months Integer 1, 12, 24, 36 Committed contract period duration. Multi-year contracts strongly protect Net Revenue Retention.
service_tier Categorical Starter, Growth, Enterprise License package tier. Enterprise tiers receive dedicated CSM support and custom SLA agreements.
monthly_charges Float $10.00 to $2,500.00 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) billed to the customer. Primary weight in financial risk calculations.
total_charges Float $10.00 to $100,000.00 Historical cumulative revenue received. Proxy for customer lifetime value (LTV).
product_usage_score Float 0.0 to 100.0 Daily Active Users (DAU) ratio and feature breadth index. Strongest negative correlation with churn.
nps_score Integer 0 to 10 Customer Net Promoter Score recorded during quarterly reviews. 0-6 signals active dissatisfaction.
support_tickets_quarter Integer 0 to 14 tickets Technical and operational tickets logged in past 90 days. > 4 indicates severe product workflow blockers.
payment_delay_days Integer 0 to 50 days Average overdue invoice payment latency. > 15 days indicates cashflow problems or billing dissatisfaction.
has_churned Binary Target 0 (Retained), 1 (Churned) Ground-truth classification target. 0 indicates active paying subscriber; 1 indicates canceled/churned account.
